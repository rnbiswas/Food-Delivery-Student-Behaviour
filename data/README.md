# Survey variables

The raw survey responses are **not** published. Participants were told their answers would stay anonymous and would not be shared.

**Sample:** 250 college students in India who had used a food delivery app in the last 6 months.

| Group | Variables |
|---|---|
| Demographics | Gender, city tier (1/2/3), living arrangement (hostel / PG / with family / alone), employment status |
| Behaviour | Preferred platform (Zomato / Swiggy / Uber Eats / other), preferred cuisine, usual ordering time, order frequency, average monthly spend (INR) |
| Context and motivation | Subscription membership (yes/no), discount dependence (high/medium/low), peer influence, primary payment method |
| Outcome | Overall satisfaction (1 = very dissatisfied to 5 = very satisfied) |

The full questionnaire is in the appendix of the report in `/report`.
